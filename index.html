    How to play

    Choose a glass, then an empty glass or a matching top color with space. Each pour moves the whole matching run that fits. Settle every color into a full glass of four.

    Free pairs can pour together; each glass stays occupied through recovery. Undo restores a previous pour. Hint points toward a legal solution; Restart begins only the current level.

    Keys 1–6 select glasses. H, U and R use Hint, Undo and Restart. Tab and Enter activate the same controls. Color names are read on each glass.

    Stars: one for completion, two within three moves of the authored target, three at the target. These are tested move goals, without an optimality claim. Hints and Undo do not invalidate stars.

    Your underway campaign board and exact Undo history stay saved.

    About STILLLIGHT

    A quiet, local water-sort candidate with thirty ordinary levels and earned cosmetic finishes.

    Your progress, profile, preferences and purchases with earned coins live in this browser on this device. No account or cloud sync. Clearing browser data removes them. Storage failures show recovery options and allow play in memory.

    Local dates follow your device calendar. Weeks begin Monday. Three distinct attended days earn a weekly claim; missing days does not erase attendance. Returning to an earlier date reuses its saved claims. Changing your clock can open another local date; this is a local feature, without online anti-cheat.
